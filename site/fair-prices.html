What should it cost?

Entry tickets have an official price, and we quote the Ministry’s own list. A camel ride or a tip has none, so for those this page shows what named guides publish, who they are, when they said it and where they disagree. Below them are the official market prices Egypt’s Cabinet publishes every day.

What the market charges residents.

From the Cabinet’s Information and Decision Support Centre, which publishes retail prices across the governorates every day. A useful check: a fruit stall asking several times the top of this range is not quoting the price a local would pay.

What every source agrees on

Three habits that matter more than any number.

Agree the total first

For rides and boats, agree the full price, how long it lasts and the trip back before you get on. Every guide cited above says this.

Anchor on the app

Before agreeing a street-taxi fare, open a ride-hailing app for the same trip and read its quote, even if you then take the taxi.

Carry small notes

Tips are expected in Egyptian pounds; big notes are hard to break. The rate above tells you what a note is worth today.
