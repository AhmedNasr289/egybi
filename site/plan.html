Plan check

Route planner · tile-free map · saved on this device, or to your account if you choose

Your route, on the map.

Add the places you want to see and the days at each. For every stop the planner shows what the data holds — the heat a normal afternoon reaches in your months, from NASA’s climate record, and the straight-line distance from the stop before. Then print it as a Papyrus dossier.

When

Your dates

Your dates, stops and name are kept in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server unless you sign in and press Save to my account; an account is free and optional.

Where

Add your stops.

Click a coloured dot on the map — each is one of eighteen measured sites, coloured by the heat in your main month — or use the buttons and the search below.

    What prints

    Your Papyrus dossier.

    This is exactly what the print button produces: a cover, one sheet per stop and the holidays inside your dates, with the sources at the foot. Use your browser’s “Save as PDF” to keep it.
