Egypt’s weather, month by month, at 18 sites.

How hot a normal afternoon gets at each site in each month. Heat is most of the planning problem in Egypt, and it depends on where as much as on when.

In January, February and December, 18 of 18 sites have a normal afternoon under 28 °C — the most of any month. In July, August and September, none does.

The hottest normal afternoon in the table is Philae Temple in August, about 43 °C.

In July the spread between sites is 13 °C: about 29 °C at Abu Mena, about 42 °C at Philae Temple. Which month to go depends on where as much as on when.

Every site stays under 34 °C in the afternoon in January, February, March, November and December.
