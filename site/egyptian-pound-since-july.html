The Egyptian pound since July: what your money buys.

How many pounds ten currencies bought, kept day by day since late July. One move, seen through ten windows.

From the first week (from 24 July 2026) to the last (to 12 November 2026), one US dollar went from EGP 51.30 to EGP 53.90: +5.1%. Put the other way, the pound lost 4.8% of its dollar value. 100 dollars bought EGP 5,130 then and EGP 5,390 now.

The Saudi riyal, the UAE dirham and the Qatari riyal moved by the same amount (+5.1% each). Their price in dollars varied by at most 0.8% over these weeks, because they are held to the dollar, so for a visitor from the Gulf it is the same move, not a second one.

The others differ by their own move against the dollar: the Chinese yuan +6.6%; sterling +5.5%; the Kuwaiti dinar +5.3%; the Canadian dollar +4.9%; the euro +3.1%; the Turkish lira −0.8%.

It was not one-way. In the week to 13 August 2026 the dollar bought 2.9% fewer pounds than in the first week (EGP 49.81): the pound strengthened before it fell.

The largest weekly move came in the week to 29 October 2026: every currency moved +2.9% to +4.6% against the pound in the same week, so that was a move of the pound itself.
