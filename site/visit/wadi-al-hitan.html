Wadi al-Hitan, month by month.

وادي الحيتان

The coolest afternoons come in January, at about 18 °C; the hottest in July, at about 37 °C.

A normal afternoon stays under 30 °C in 7 months: January, February, March, April, October, November, December.

The ground at the site's coordinates sits 143 m above sea level (NASA SRTM).

It is part of a UNESCO World Heritage property.
