دير سانت كاترين

Today at Saint Catherine's Monastery

Sunrise, golden light and sunset for the 15th of every month are in the light table further down this page. With JavaScript on, today’s times and how long is left appear here.

Sun times are worked out in your browser for this site’s coordinates, with the NOAA solar equations used for the light table, and shown in Egypt time from your browser’s own time-zone database. Cliffs, dunes and temple walls can hide a low sun earlier. Temperatures: NASA POWER climatology (MERRA-2 reanalysis) normals, 2001-2020.

The coolest afternoons come in January, at about 17 °C; the hottest in August, at about 36 °C.

A normal afternoon stays under 30 °C in 7 months: January, February, March, April, October, November, December.

Opening hours: The monastery: 08:45 to 12:45, closed on Sundays. For Fridays the list prints 10:30 am to 11:30 pm, which looks like a misprint, so check the Friday hours locally. The afternoon temperatures below are what a visit after midday meets. Source: Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities price list (5 Nov 2024): St Catherine's Monastery, checked 17 November 2026.

The ground at the site's coordinates sits 1586 m above sea level (NASA SRTM).

It is part of a UNESCO World Heritage property.
