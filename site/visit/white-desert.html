The White Desert, month by month.

الصحراء البيضاء

The coolest afternoons come in January, at about 19 °C; the hottest in August, at about 38 °C.

A normal afternoon stays under 30 °C in 5 months: January, February, March, November, December.

The ground at the site's coordinates sits 58 m above sea level (NASA SRTM).

Light for photographs

Golden light, the warm low sun photographers wait for, lasts about 31–35 minutes after sunrise and before sunset here, depending on the month.
