Plan check

Plan check · written in Cairo · from the data on this site

Have your plan read by someone in Cairo.

Send your draft itinerary. Within 48 hours you get back a written check of the things that go wrong most often — heat, distances, holidays, prices — each point tied to a sourced figure, the way everything on this site is.

The plan check
$49

per trip, one written review and one round of follow-up questions

  • Heat: each day against the measured afternoon at that site in your month, and where the hours should move.
  • Distances: whether the legs between stops fit the days you gave them.
  • Holidays: what falls inside your dates, and what it changes.
  • Prices: the tips, entry and rides on your route, against the published figures and today’s rate.
What it is not
  • No bookings, no tickets and no commission from anyone you are sent to.
  • Not guiding, and not advice on visas, health or safety — for those, the official sources.
  • Nothing invented: where the data cannot answer a question, the review says so.
How paying works You email first. We reply within 48 hours to confirm we can help with your trip and send a payment link. Nothing is charged before that reply, and if we cannot help, we say so.
