Egypt in orbit

Return the name, launch_year and partner of every satellite, earliest launch first.

Solve this challenge →

Runs in your browser. No signup needed.

What you are given

satellites(name, launch_year, purpose, partner).

Starting point

SELECT name, launch_year, partner
FROM satellites
ORDER BY launch_year;

The reference solution is checked automatically when you run your query on the practice page — results are compared row by row, and order matters for this one.

Did you know

Egypt has launched satellites since 2007 with partners spanning Ukraine, Russia, France, China and Germany — and now hosts the African Space Agency. (Space in Africa)
