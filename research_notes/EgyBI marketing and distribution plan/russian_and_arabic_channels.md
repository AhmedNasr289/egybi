## Russian market: size, audience and platform conditions (Telegram, YouTube, VK, Instagram, payments, ad law)

### Takeaway
Russian demand for Egypt in 2026 is at a record and still growing (about +24% year on year). But Russian distribution itself is fragmented. Telegram, the natural channel for Hurghada/Sharm chats, has been throttled and partly blocked since February 2026 and now works mainly over VPN. YouTube is throttled. The state messenger Max and VK are pushed as the replacements. Any paid placement aimed at Russians is legally "advertising" and needs ERID labelling. For a free, non-commercial site, earned and native posting is the realistic route.

**Advertising law (ERID)**
- Almost all internet advertising in Russia, Telegram included, must be registered with an ad-data operator (ORD) and carry an `erid` token before publication. Each creative needs its own erid. Unlabelled ads can be fined up to ₽500,000 (legal entities). — [kokoc.com](https://kokoc.com/blog/markirovka-reklamy); [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/marketing/2853068-markirovka-reklamy-v-telegram); [companies.rbc.ru checklist](https://companies.rbc.ru/news/tg8J6Cvjc7/markirovka-reklamyi-v-telegram-2026-polnyij-chek-list-dlya-marketologa/)
- Reportedly, FAS said it would not fine the mere fact of advertising in Telegram until end-2026, but labelling and the advertising levy (рекламный сбор) obligations still apply. — [kurs.alfabank.ru](https://kurs.alfabank.ru/articles/reklama-v-telegrame/)
- FAS collected ₽13.08M in fines under KoAP art. 14.3 in the first 5 months of 2026. — [sostav.ru blog](https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/285528/85619)

**Payments (what Russians can and cannot buy)**
- Mir cards and Russian-issued Visa/Mastercard do not work in Egypt (sanctions since 2022). Some Russian UnionPay cards may work, but not reliably. Tourists rely on cash USD/EUR, foreign-bank cards and QR payments: VTB, and reportedly Sber, launched QR payment in Egypt (news dated 22 Apr 2026). — [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/tehrevizor/2684207-rossiyskie-karty-v-egipte); [tourdom.ru on QR](https://www.tourdom.ru/news/turisty-v-egipte-teper-mogut-rasplachivatsya-po-qr-kodu.html); [travelto.ru winter 2026–27](https://travelto.ru/blog/kak-platit-za-granice-zimo-20262027-egipet-tailand-i-oae/); [dp.ru, 22 Apr 2026](https://www.dp.ru/a/2026/04/22/otpusk-na-kuarah-kak-banki)

### Inferences
- Because Telegram now needs a VPN for many users and Max is being pushed, plan for multiple homes: a Telegram channel (still the place where Hurghada/Sharm tourists ask questions), a mirrored Max channel, and VK. Each channel is a few minutes of reposting per week.
- EgyBI is free and sells nothing, so its own posts in chats are not "advertising" in the commercial sense. But **paying** any Russian channel for a post creates an ad that needs an erid via an ORD, plus the levy. This is impractical for a solo creator in Egypt, so avoid paid seeding in Russia. Rely on earned mentions: channel admins citing the fair-prices page as a useful resource.
- Do not monetise Russian traffic with anything Russians cannot pay for. Card payments from Russian cards fail, so affiliate links to Western booking sites convert poorly. If monetising at all, use Tripster/Sputnik8, which take rouble payments.
- The "money" content (which cards work, cash USD, QR payment, visa $30 cash) is high-value for the /ru/ arriving page and changes often, which suits a "fresh, dated" positioning.

### Inferences
- **Tactic for TravelAsk/"live" chats:** the questions that recur in these chats (how much to tip, is an excursion price fair, which SIM, how to pay) are exactly EgyBI's pages. The native move is to answer the question in-thread with the actual number (e.g. "Luxor from Hurghada, fair range X–Y USD, entry tickets Z; source page: egybi.com/ru/…"). Do not post a standalone promo. Many such chats run anti-spam bots that delete links from new members, so build a few weeks of link-free helpful replies first. Rules vary per chat and were not verified.
- **Channel admins** (e.g. Hurghada guide channels) are often excursion sellers themselves, so a "fair prices" page can look like competition. Pitch EgyBI as neutral reference data, e.g. a monthly "EGP rate + entry ticket prices" card they can repost.
- **Forums (forum.awd.ru, tophotels.ru, tourister.ru):** no 2026 rules found. From background knowledge (unverified): Vinsky forum is strict about advertising and allows links mainly in a signature or as genuinely useful references after an account has history, and TopHotels is hotel-review centred. The safe pattern is detailed trip reports with prices (отчёт о поездке), linking the planner once.
- **Otzovik/Irecommend:** review sites. A "review" of your own site would count as self-promotion. Low priority.
- **Tripster/Sputnik8:** EgyBI does not sell excursions, but the Tripster affiliate programme could monetise Russian traffic in roubles. That would make the fair-prices page look less neutral, so keep any affiliate link clearly labelled or skip it.
- **YouTube/RuTube/VK Video vloggers:** no names were found in this search. Short videos (VK Clips, YouTube Shorts) showing "the real price of a Luxor trip" are the format most likely to travel.

## Arabic market: Gulf/Saudi visitors, Egyptian domestic audience, platforms

### Takeaway
Saudi Arabia is a large and growing source market: about 425k Saudis in Q1 2026, with roughly 1.2M estimated for 2025 and 1.7M projected for 2026 (estimates). Saudi summer travel shifted toward New Alamein/North Coast, with new direct Saudia flights. Saudi audiences are Snapchat-first. Egypt's Arabic audience is Facebook-first, with about 60M+ users, and travel there is organised through very large Facebook groups.

### Inferences
- **Saudi/Gulf playbook:** Saudis visiting Egypt increasingly go to Alamein/North Coast in summer and to Cairo for city breaks. EgyBI's heat-per-site and "when are you going" briefing fits the Saudi summer question («أفضل وقت لزيارة مصر», whether Luxor is too hot in August).
