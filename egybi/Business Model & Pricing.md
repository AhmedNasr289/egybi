# Business Model & Pricing

## Recommendation: freemium, dual-currency

Every real competitor in [[Competitive Landscape]] runs freemium (free core practice, paid premium tracks/certs) — DataCamp $14/mo billed annually ($168/yr) or $35/mo monthly, LeetCode $35/mo or ~$159/yr, DataLemur $15/mo or $60/yr. That model fits here too.

**Free tier:** SQL Fundamentals track in full, limited daily practice on other tracks, core gamification (ranks/badges/streaks).
**Paid tier:** full access to all 9 tracks, advanced modern-data-stack challenges (the actual whitespace identified in [[Competitive Landscape]]), certificates, and — once built — the Python/Spark/dbt/Airflow execution tier.

## Pricing — Egypt/MENA vs. global

Given the explicit ambition to help "all data engineers and BI developers" globally, while keeping a genuine Egypt-first identity, recommend **purchasing-power-adjusted regional pricing** rather than one flat global price — none of the eight competitors researched do this, so it's a real, differentiating goodwill gesture for the Egypt/MENA audience, consistent with the EGP-first pricing approach already used for EGYBi.

- **Egypt/MENA tier:** priced in EGP, meaningfully below the global-USD-equivalent list price (mirroring the Launch-vs-Standard logic from EGYBi's pricing).
- **Global tier:** priced in USD, benchmarked just under DataCamp/LeetCode's ~$14–15/mo to undercut the closest comps.

Exact figures should be set once the [[Build Plan|Phase 0]] validation step gauges real willingness to pay — don't lock numbers before that.

## Why not HackerRank's model
HackerRank's real revenue is B2B recruiting/assessment tooling, not individual subscriptions — a plausible Phase 2+ expansion (enterprise teams wanting to assess data-engineering candidates) once EgyBI has a content library and reputation, but not a Phase 1 bet.
