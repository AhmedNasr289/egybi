## 1. The verdict

**A website is not the vehicle for this target. Selling your skills is.**

- **No website built from zero traffic reaches $1,000 a month in 3–6 months, and egybi.com is not an exception.** At ~37 visitors a week, ads and affiliate links earn roughly nothing, and every product route (data subscription, prints, templates) needs either an audience or B2B selling, both of which take longer than six months.
- **The fastest route is selling your data and BI skills where buyers already are: Upwork, with Fiverr in parallel.** Both pay out through Payoneer, which works in Egypt. Neither needs capital, traffic, a payment gateway or a shipping route, which removes every blocker the dropshipping research found.
- **What to do with egybi.com:** keep it and stop writing new encyclopaedia content for 90 days. Turn it into your **portfolio**, with one new `hire.html` page and two Power BI case studies built from its own data. The price and FX pipelines keep running unattended on their crons, so the archive keeps growing at no cost.

**The base case below crosses $1,000 net in month 4 and reaches ~$1,900 net in month 6. Confidence that the combined plan reaches ≥$1,000 net by month 6: 0.60.**

⚠️ **Before anything else, check your employment contract.** Moonlighting, IP-assignment and non-compete clauses are common in BI roles. If yours forbids paid outside work, or claims IP for work in your field, this plan is dead on day one (see §5).

## 3. The choice

**Primary: #1, Upwork, positioned as "Power BI dashboards and automated data pipelines — English/Arabic, tested and documented."**
**Cheap parallel: #2, Fiverr productised gigs.** It costs $0, buyers come to you through Fiverr search, and reviews compound into ranking.

**Why #1 beats #3 (Upwork data engineering):** same platform, higher rates, but the jobs are bigger and slower to land without reviews. Dashboard jobs are smaller and more frequent, so they build the review count that later wins the engineering work. The plan folds #3 in from month 2 rather than leading with it.

**Why #1 beats #4 (direct Gulf B2B):** no fees and better rates, but a stranger with no reviews has nothing to show a Gulf ops manager. Upwork reviews plus the egybi.com portfolio become that proof, so #4 starts in month 3 using it. That is also the route off the platform's fee over time.

**Competing hypotheses:**
- Skills on platforms is the fastest route to $1,000: **0.60**.
- Direct B2B is faster, if you already have a network I don't know about: **0.15**.
- Vetted networks: **0.05**.
- Any website or asset route: **0.05**.
- Something I haven't considered: **0.15**.

## 4. Execution plan

### Positioning — what makes you more than another $20 dashboard seller

- **Bilingual EN/AR dashboards**, right-to-left included. ⚠️ Demand from Gulf and Egyptian SMEs is an **assumption to check**: track how many Arabic-tagged jobs you see per week.
- **"Tested and documented":** your verification habit, where every number has a source and a checker proves the work, is a real selling point in a market of fragile dashboards.
- **End-to-end delivery:** you can deliver the pipeline as well as the visual, including a scheduled refresh. That is the step up to $40+/h.
- **Avoid workforce-management and contact-centre analytics** unless your contract clears it. It is your strongest niche and the most likely to overlap with your employer.

### Fees, so the arithmetic is honest

- **Upwork service fee:** variable 0–15% since 2025-05-01, **typically ~10%**.
- **Connects:** **$0.15 each**, with a proposal costing 6–16 Connects (~$1.80 average assumed). You get 10 free Connects a month.
- **Payoneer:** ~1% to receive plus ~2–3.5% conversion to EGP. Net factor used below: **0.90 × 0.965 = 0.8685**.
- **Fiverr:** a flat **20%**, plus a $1–3 Payoneer withdrawal fee and ~2% conversion. Net factor used: **0.80 × 0.98 = 0.784**.

## 5. Attacking the plan

**The strongest case against it:**

> "This is trading hours for money on top of a full-time job. Upwork is a race to the bottom: 7% reply rates, competing with $8/h listings from Egypt itself. You'll hit $1,900 at 18 hours a week, at the edge of burnout, with no asset growing underneath, while the price archive, your one compounding asset, gets no attention. A real founder builds something that earns while they sleep."

**The answer:**

- **The target is $1,000–2,000 a month, fast.** Nothing that earns while you sleep reaches that in six months from 37 visitors a week; the research in [[Dropshipping — Can It, Should It]] and the two earlier plans shows that.
- **The archive isn't abandoned.** Its pipelines run unattended, and it grows on its own.
- **Freelancing builds the two things the asset routes lack: cash and proof.** Reviews and a client list are exactly what the B2B data-subscription plan needs before anyone will buy from you.
- **The hour cap is real, which is why the plan raises rates at every step rather than adding hours.**

**One concrete reason to reject this plan:**

> **If your employment contract forbids paid outside work, or assigns your employer IP in work done in your field, this plan is dead on day one**, and that clause is common in BI roles. In that case the only legal routes left are passive ones (Etsy prints, templates), none of which reach $1,000 a month within six months. The honest choices then are to renegotiate the clause, or to accept a slower asset plan.

## Assumptions to check before acting

1. **Your employment contract allows paid outside work and doesn't claim this IP.** This gates everything.
2. Upwork → Payoneer → Egyptian bank works for your account, at the fees shown in your Upwork withdrawal settings. Several sources say it does; none of them is official for Egypt.
3. Fiverr's Payoneer withdrawal works for Egypt. Fiverr's help page lists the method but not the countries.
4. The actual Upwork fee on your contracts (0–15% variable; ~10% assumed).
5. Demand for Arabic/RTL dashboards. Count the matching Upwork jobs per week in weeks 1–2.
6. Egyptian tax treatment of freelance income. Ask an accountant before month 3.
7. Payoneer's $29.95 annual fee applies if you receive under $2,000 a year; you would exceed that by month 4.

## Self-check

- **The chosen route can pay you in Egypt:** yes. Upwork and Fiverr both pay through Payoneer, which multiple 2026 sources confirm works in Egypt, and no Stripe, Shopify Payments or PayPal is needed. The remaining risk is listed as assumptions 2–3.
- **The monthly numbers add up:** yes. Every row uses the stated net factors, month 6 is shown worked out, the six-month total is 40 + 410 + 701 + 1,158 + 1,550 + 1,899 = **$5,758**, and hours fit the 15–20 h budget every month.
- **Is a website the right vehicle at all:** answered first, and the answer is no — for this target and timeline the vehicle is selling your skills. egybi.com becomes the portfolio, not the income.
