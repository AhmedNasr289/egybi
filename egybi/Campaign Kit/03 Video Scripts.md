# 03 Video Scripts

> Status: three faceless scripts ready to record, written 2026-11-19; record them in the social-batch block of weeks 1–5. Back to [[Home]] · plan: reports/EgyBI marketing and distribution plan.md · links follow [[01 Phase 0 — Listing, Search Setup and UTM]].

**How to make each one.** Record the screen of a phone (or a desktop browser set to a phone-sized window, 1080×1920) with the built-in screen recorder, on the live egybi.com pages named in each shot. Add the on-screen text in CapCut, InShot or Canva, and record the voiceover with your own voice. One file goes to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

**Assets.** Screen recordings of egybi.com and nothing else: no stock footage, no AI-generated images or video of monuments, no music with lyrics. If you want a photo of a temple, use one you took yourself.

**AI disclosure.** These scripts use only screen recordings and your own voice, so no AI label is needed. If you use an AI-generated voice instead of your own, switch on the platform's AI-generated content label when you upload (TikTok: "AI-generated content" toggle under More options; YouTube: "Altered or synthetic content" = Yes; Instagram: "AI info" label).

**Links.** TikTok and Instagram: the bio link (see each script). YouTube Shorts: links in a Short's description are not clickable, so put the link in the channel's bio links and say "link on my channel". Bio links can be changed per script while that video is the newest.

## (b) "Your name in hieroglyphs — comment yours" — 25 seconds

**Page:** https://egybi.com/hieroglyphs.html. Before recording, decide the demo name (your own name works best). Then make the reply videos: one 10-second recording per commented name, typing it in and holding on the cartouche.

| Time | Shot (screen capture) | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | hieroglyphs.html, empty name box | **Your name in hieroglyphs** | "This is how your name looks in hieroglyphs." |
| 3–9 s | Type the demo name letter by letter; the cartouche fills in | **Sign by sign, from Gardiner's sign list** | "Each sound gets the sign Egyptologists use for it, inside a cartouche, the oval that held royal names." |
| 9–15 s | Scroll to "What each sign is" with Gardiner numbers | **Every sign has its Gardiner number** | "Every sign is listed with its number and what it shows." |
| 15–21 s | Scroll to "It spells the sounds, not the meaning" | **It spells the sounds. It is not a translation.** | "To be honest about it: this spells the sounds of your name. It's not how an ancient scribe would have written it, and it's not a translation." |
| 21–25 s | Back to the cartouche; tap "Save as an image" | **Comment your name, I'll do the next ten** | "Comment your name and I'll make the next ten. I built this; it's free, no signup." |

**Caption:**

> Your name in hieroglyphs, spelled sign by sign with each sign's Gardiner number. It spells the sounds of your name; it's a transliteration, not a translation, and the page explains why. Comment your name and I'll do the next ten. Free tool I built.
> #hieroglyphs #ancientegypt #egypt #egyptology #yourname #cartouche

**Pinned comment:**

> Signs from A. H. Gardiner's sign list (Egyptian Grammar, 3rd ed., 1957), using the single-sound ("uniliteral") signs. Ancient Egyptian wrote consonants and added meaning signs, so this sound-by-sound spelling is a modern convention, not how a scribe would have written your name. Drop your name below, Latin letters.

**Reply videos:** use TikTok's "Reply with video" on each name comment. The on-screen text is the commenter's own name followed by ", in hieroglyphs"; for example, replying to Sara, it reads **"Sara, in hieroglyphs"**. Voiceover: "Sara, here's yours. It spells the sounds, not a translation."
