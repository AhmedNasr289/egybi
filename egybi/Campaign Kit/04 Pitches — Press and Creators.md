# 04 Pitches — Press and Creators

> Status: ten complete pitches for Ahmed to review and send from his own email, written 2026-11-19; send two a week from week 4 (14 December 2026). Back to [[Home]] · plan: reports/EgyBI marketing and distribution plan.md · links follow [[01 Phase 0 — Listing, Search Setup and UTM]].

Rules for every pitch:

- One finding, two or three numbers, a free chart or table, one offer. Under 150 words in the body.
- Send to the one reporter or creator who covers the subject, never to a general desk list.
- Before sending, re-check the figures in the pitch against the live page; prices and rates move.
- No payment in either direction. Never pay a Russian channel or creator for a post (ERID law).
- Greeting: each email opens "Hello," as written. When you have the person's name, change it to "Hello" followed by their first name; nothing else in the email needs editing except the one personal sentence where a creator pitch asks for it.
- Follow up once, seven days later, with one line: "Following up in case this is useful for your coverage; happy to send the data or a quote." Then stop.
- Log each send (date, outlet, person, angle, link) in the outreach sheet.

**One-line bio used in every press pitch:** Ahmed, Cairo-based data engineer. I built and run EgyBI (egybi.com), a free English, Arabic and Russian guide to Egypt where every figure carries its source and date. hello@egybi.com
