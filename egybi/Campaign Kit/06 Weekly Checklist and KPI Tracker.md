## 1. The 7-hour week, as a checklist

Copy this block into the week's note each Monday. Claude prepares every draft before the block starts; your hours go to judgement, credibility and pressing publish.

### Monday — metrics and plan (0.5 h)
- [ ] Read last week's numbers into the KPI row below (visitors, planner prints, subscribers, sales, referring domains).
- [ ] Every second Monday: Search Console readings for Last 3 months and Last 28 days ([[01 Phase 0 — Listing, Search Setup and UTM]] §3).
- [ ] Pick the week's hook from [[07 Content Calendar.csv]] and tick which rows you will post.

### Asset of the week (1.0 h)
- [ ] Review the Claude PR for the week's page or data refresh; open the changed pages on your phone.
- [ ] Check one figure on the page against its source link.
- [ ] Merge, or leave a comment saying what to change.

### English community answers (1.0 h)
- [ ] Answer 3–4 real Reddit questions in your own words, no links (first five weeks: no links anywhere on Reddit).
- [ ] Answer 2 TripAdvisor Cairo or Luxor forum questions; link only neutral official sources; EgyBI stays in your profile.
- [ ] Keep promotion under one in ten of everything the account posts.

### Russian answers and the weekly card (1.0 h)
- [ ] Answer 5–8 questions in 4–6 Hurghada and Sharm chats with the figure and its source.
- [ ] Post the weekly card (rate, a ticket, heat) on the EgyBI Telegram channel; mirror to VK and Max.
- [ ] No paid posts, ever.

### Social batch (1.25 h)
- [ ] Record two screen-capture voiceovers from [[03 Video Scripts]] or the calendar's video rows.
- [ ] Upload the same file to TikTok, Reels and Shorts; pin the source comment.
- [ ] Approve the week's pins and the LinkedIn post.

### Outreach (1.0 h)
- [ ] Send two press or creator pitches from [[04 Pitches — Press and Creators]], each personalised.
- [ ] Send one follow-up to a pitch from seven days ago, then close it in the log.
- [ ] One partner email (hostel, guesthouse or guide) offering the QR card.

### Arabic and newsletter (0.75 h)
- [ ] One Arabic Facebook group post, admin asked first, link in the first comment.
- [ ] In issue weeks: read and approve The Papyrus, then send.

### Buffer (0.5 h)
- [ ] Reply to comments and replies from the week.
- [ ] Anything unexpected.

**Total: 7.0 h.** In the Eid week (8 March) and the Christmas week (21 December), do only the Monday block, the Russian card and the buffer.

## 4. The Day-84 rule (1 January 2027) and the 31 March checkpoint

The rule stays as the plan set it:

- **Run-rate ≥ $100 a month → continue** into the peak season.
- **Run-rate < $30 a month → stop building.** "Stop building" means no new features; distribution of the existing pages continues on a 3-hour maintenance rhythm: the Russian card, Reddit answers, the monthly newsletter and price refreshes.
- **$30–100 (the grey zone) → keep marketing at the full 7 hours with no new features** until 31 March, then apply the same thresholds.

Two clarifications from the plan: measure run-rate over the **trailing 14 days since checkout went live**, scaled to a month (× 30 ÷ 14), because a store that opens on 29 November has only about 33 days before the check. And write the verdict into this note on 1 January, with the numbers, before deciding anything else.

**31 March 2027:** ≥ $150 a month → keep the 7-hour plan through the season and consider affiliates (labelled, and never on the fair-prices page). Below $50 a month **and** fewer than 300 visitors a week → maintenance mode, re-check on 1 October 2027.
