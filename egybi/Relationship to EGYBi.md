# History — From "Per Ankh" to EgyBI, and Why the Domain Is EGYBi.com

**This platform was designed and researched under the working name "Per Ankh"; Ahmed renamed it to EgyBI on 2026-07-18.** This note preserves that history so future sessions don't get confused finding "Per Ankh" referenced in older material (commit messages, earlier vault versions, etc.) — treat "Per Ankh" and "EgyBI" as the same project across time, with EgyBI as the current, correct name.

## Timeline

1. **Originally:** Ahmed was building two separate SaaS ventures — **EGYBi.com** (a BI/analytics SaaS for Egyptian BPOs, concept "EGYBi WFM") and a second, sibling project then called **Per Ankh** (this gamified DE/BI practice platform), sharing some billing/infrastructure patterns.
2. **2026-07-18:** Ahmed dropped EGYBi WFM entirely and decided Per Ankh would be his sole SaaS venture. Since EGYBi WFM's domain (`egybi.com`, already registered and Hostinger-hosted) was now unused, he reused it for Per Ankh rather than buying a new domain (`perankh.io`/`perankh.dev`/`houseoflife.dev` had been the earlier suggestions — now moot).
3. **2026-07-18 (later, same day):** Ahmed renamed the product itself from "Per Ankh" to **EgyBI**, matching the domain. The mascot, rank ladder, badges, curriculum, and all creative identity in [[Brand & Creative Identity]] carried over unchanged — only the name changed.

## What this means today

- **EgyBI is Ahmed's only active SaaS project.** There is no other venture to share infrastructure with or sequence builds behind — see [[Build Plan]] and [[Risks & Mitigations]], both already updated to drop the "two ventures" framing.
- **The old EGYBi WFM plan/vault** (`C:\dev\egybi\vault`, plan at `C:\Users\okasha.31\.claude\plans\you-are-a-senior-compiled-dawn.md`) is kept as historical reference only, clearly marked DROPPED. Its site files (`C:\dev\egybi\site\wfm\`, `demos\`) were **deleted** at Ahmed's request on 2026-07-18 — that concept isn't just dormant, it's gone.
- **"House of Life" survives as EgyBI's guiding metaphor**, even though it's no longer the literal brand name — the tagline, the rank ladder culminating in "Keeper of the House of Life," and the overall narrative frame are unaffected by the rename.
