## 1. The verdict

**egybi.com cannot earn $300 a month from its own traffic within a year.** Confidence that a traffic-led plan reaches $300 by month 12: **0.08**. The arithmetic:

| What would have to be true | Requirement | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| Ads on Egyptian traffic | **$300 ÷ $1 RPM × 1,000 = 300,000 pageviews/month.** At the best Egypt RPM ($2.50), still 120,000 | ~160 visits/month today. Egypt RPM is **$0.30–2.50** (Brightseotools, 2026) |
| Ads on tourist traffic | $300 ÷ ~$15 RPM × 1,000 = **20,000 pageviews/month** (the $15 is an assumption, not sourced) | Would need ~125× today's traffic |
| Ranking new pages to get that traffic | — | **Only 1.74% of newly published pages reach Google's top 10 within a year**, and 72.9% of top-10 pages are over 3 years old (Ahrefs) |

**What does reach $300–400 is a stack of three routes, with egybi.com as the storefront rather than the traffic source:**

1. **Egypt data and map services**, sold through Fiverr and a new `services.html`. This is the fast route and carries most of the income.
2. **Egypt map prints and printable PDFs on Etsy.** Etsy's own search brings the buyers, not the site.
3. **Travel affiliate links (plain links, no scripts)** on 18 new "best time to visit" pages generated from the NASA climate data you already have. Small at first, and it grows with the site.

**Base case: crosses $300 net in month 4, sits in the $300–400 band in months 4–7, and reaches ~$585 by month 12. Confidence ≥$300 by month 6: 0.50; by month 12: 0.65.**

⚠️ The same gate as in the other income plan applies: **check your employment contract for outside-work and IP clauses first.** Route 1 carries the stack.

## 3. The stack, and why

| Layer | Role | Share of month-12 income |
|---|---|---|
| **#1 Services** | Gets to $300 fastest: ~3 orders a month is the whole target on its own | ~60% |
| **#3 Etsy prints + PDFs** | Semi-passive; listings keep selling while you sleep, and every print carries the egybi.com name | ~31% |
| **#4 Travel affiliate pages** | Small now; the one layer that grows with the site's own traffic | ~9% |

**Why not #7–#9 (ads, Arabic price pages):** the arithmetic in §1. Adding ad scripts would also give up the site's zero-third-party discipline in exchange for about $1–5 a month. **Revisit only if** tourist traffic passes ~5,000 sessions a month, which is above Journey's 1,000 threshold and high enough to actually earn something.

**Why not #2 (hotel maps) as a pillar:** one sale at $2,400 beats the monthly target eight times over, but it depends on outbound sales to hotel GMs, which doesn't fit 6–10 hours a week alongside everything else. **Keep it as an upsell**: any services client who is a hotel or DMC gets offered it. That gives you upside without building the plan on it.

**Competing hypotheses:**
- The services-led stack reaches $300 by month 6: **0.50**.
- A traffic-led plan (SEO plus ads or affiliates) reaches $300 by month 12: **0.08**.
- One big-ticket B2B sale lands within 12 months: **0.20**. It isn't planned for; it is captured if it comes.

## 7. Attacking the plan

**The strongest case against it:**

> "You asked how to make the *website* earn $300 a month, and the answer is 'mostly do freelance work on Fiverr.' That isn't the website earning anything. The day you stop delivering orders, 60% of the income stops. The site's own traffic plays almost no part, and in a year you'll still have ~200 visits a month."

**The answer:**

- **That's a fair description, and the arithmetic in §1 is why.** No honest plan gets a 37-visitor-a-week site to $300 from its own traffic within a year. Any plan that claims to is selling you a projection, and this site exists to refuse those.
- **The site still does real work in the stack.** It is the portfolio that makes a $150 gig credible, the storefront for prints, and the home of 18 new pages that build the only traffic able to replace services income later.
- **Etsy and affiliate links are the semi-passive part**, about 40% of the month-12 total, and they keep growing when you stop delivering services.

**One concrete reason to reject this plan:**

> **If your employment contract forbids paid outside work, route #1 is gone**, and the stack falls to Etsy plus affiliate links: **about $119 a month at month 6 ($104 + $15) and $232 at month 12 ($182 + $50)**, below the $300 target for the whole year. In that case the only honest answer is that egybi.com cannot reach $300 a month within 12 months with what's allowed. The choice is between renegotiating the clause and accepting a slower path.
