## 1. Verdict

**The fastest $300–400 comes from selling your skills and time through marketplaces that already have buyers. egybi.com's job is the portfolio and storefront, not the traffic source.**

Three findings changed the picture since the last plan:

1. **Search traffic is getting harder, not easier.**
   - In a field study, AI Overviews cut organic clicks by **38%** (Search Engine Journal).
   - Seer Interactive measured position-1 CTR falling about **52%** on queries that show an AI Overview.
   - Only **1.74%** of new pages reach Google's top 10 within a year (Ahrefs).
   - So any plan that waits on SEO is now slower than it was in 2024.
2. **AI-training work is open to Egypt residents, and it pays.**
   - Outlier lists Egyptian Arabic work at up to **$7.50/h**, and other Arabic dialects (e.g. Levantine, Najdi) at up to **$12.50/h**. Its coding/SQL tracks are listed higher.
   - Outlier pays through **Airtm**, which supports withdrawals in Egypt.
   - Alignerr ($20–40/h, one aggregator listing), OpenTrain ($15/h) and Mercor ($25–53/h, specialised) also list Arabic or expert roles.
   - **DataAnnotation excludes Egypt.**
3. **Selling digital products internationally from Egypt is possible.** Lemon Squeezy (bank payouts), Paddle (Egypt not on its restricted list) and Payhip via **PayTabs** all cover Egypt. That corrects the earlier claim that no gateway exists: the earlier claim holds only for physical goods.

**The recommended stack:**

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| **A. AI-training tasks** (Outlier / Alignerr / OpenTrain) | Fastest first dollar (1–3 weeks), but volume swings |
| **B. Fixed-price BI services** (Fiverr, Upwork, Mostaql) | The steady core; egybi.com is the portfolio |
| **C. Tutoring** SQL / Power BI / Excel (or Egyptian Arabic) on italki | Bookable hours, paid via Payoneer |
| **D. Digital products** (Power BI templates, an SQL interview pack, Egypt data packs) via Payhip+PayTabs or Lemon Squeezy | The semi-passive layer that grows |

The metric is **net dollars earned per hour worked, by layer**, reviewed every Sunday. Move hours to the best layer.

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Outlier queue empty for 2+ weeks | Move those hours to Upwork proposals or the Alignerr/OpenTrain applications |

| No Fiverr order by day 56 despite impressions | Change titles and images, $120 entry |

## 7. Attacking the plan

**The strongest objection:** "This is three side jobs and a template shop. AI-training work is gig labour with no job security; Outlier is known for queues vanishing overnight, and Egyptian Arabic pays the least of the dialects."

**The answer:**
- All true. That is why AI training is **the on-ramp, not the pillar**: it is the biggest line in months 2–4 and is deliberately cut from month 5 as services take over.
- The month-12 income is **53% services, 20% products, 12% tutoring and 15% AI training**. That mix survives any one platform failing (see the stress cases).
- And it is honest: no traffic-dependent route gets an Egypt-based site to $300 within a year, and this site refuses projections it can't back.

**One concrete reason to reject the plan:** it needs **about 9–10 hours a week of evening work on top of a full-time job**, every week, for a year. If that time doesn't exist, the realistic version is a single layer: services only, which reaches ~$235/month by month 5 on 3 hours a week.
